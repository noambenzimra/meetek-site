Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 12, 2026

At Meetek we want everyone to be able to use our website and reach us easily. The site is built to the Israeli standard IS 5568, which is based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we did to make the site accessible

  • The site is available in Hebrew (right to left) and English, with the page language set correctly.
  • The whole site can be used with a keyboard alone. There is a "Skip to content" link and a clear focus outline.
  • Headings follow a logical hierarchy, and every button and interactive element has a clear label for screen readers.
  • Text and button color contrast meets level AA, in both light and dark mode.
  • The site respects device settings: with "reduce motion" enabled, animations are turned off.
  • The site works on phones and with browser text zoom up to 200%.
  • We do not use an automatic accessibility overlay. Accessibility is built into the site's code.

Known limitations

The site has been checked with automated testing tools. Full manual testing with screen readers (NVDA and VoiceOver) has not been completed yet. If anything is hard to use, please tell us and we will fix it.

Report an accessibility issue or request an accommodation

Found something that isn't accessible to you, or need information in another format? Contact us in any of these ways and we will get back to you as soon as possible:

  • Email: noam@meetek.ai
  • Phone: 058-4001054
  • WhatsApp: 058-4001054

To help us fix it quickly, please tell us which page it was on, what you were trying to do, and which device and browser you used.

Accessibility of our service

  • The intro meeting can take place at your office, by phone or by video call, whichever suits you.
  • Any document or proposal we send can be provided in an accessible format on request.
